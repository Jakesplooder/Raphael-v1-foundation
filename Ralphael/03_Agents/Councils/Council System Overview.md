# Council System Overview

Raphael councils coordinate specialized agents. Councils discuss, recommend, and delegate tasks, but do not execute actions or approve work. Aaron remains the final decision-maker.

- **Executive Council**: High-level direction, priorities, risk, resource allocation, and final recommendations.
- **Product Council**: Product vision, market fit, requirements, MVP scope, user needs, and success metrics.
- **Engineering Council**: Technical architecture, implementation planning, testing, deployment, and AI integration.
- **Operations Council**: Timeline, sprint planning, blockers, coordination, milestones, and delivery.
- **Research Council**: Market research, technical research, competitor analysis, academic research, and source gathering.
- **Career Council**: Resume, portfolio, interview prep, job readiness, and career strategy.
- **Business Council**: Business ideas, revenue models, client strategy, pricing, offers, and operations.
- **Commerce Council**: Product-based business planning for Etsy, Shopify, KDP, digital products, and print-on-demand.
- **Agency Council**: Client-service offer design, proposal planning, delivery planning, and agency operations.
- **Creator Council**: Content strategy, AI influencer brands, ebooks, newsletters, audience growth, and content funnels.
- **Financial Council**: Financial tracking, budget awareness, revenue visibility, profit review, and financial risk monitoring.
- **Portfolio Council**: Multi-business portfolio prioritization, strategic focus, roadmap balance, and opportunity comparison.
- **Governance Council**: Safety boundaries, approvals, policy, identity rules, controlled execution, and escalation oversight.
