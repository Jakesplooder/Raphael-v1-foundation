# Asset & Brand Library

Raphael's reusable creative operating system for brands, assets, prompts, templates, design systems, and relationships.

## Reuse Flow

Brand → Assets → Templates → Prompts → Designs → Content → Listings → Reuse

## Safety

- Originals are never modified.
- Imports and exports require confirmation.
- Assets remain local.
- Image contents are not stored in memory.
- External upload, publishing, cloud synchronization, credentials, and spending are disabled.
