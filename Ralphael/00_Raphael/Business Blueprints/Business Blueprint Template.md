# Business Blueprint Template

## Required Sections

- Blueprint ID
- Business name
- Business type
- One-sentence concept
- Target customer
- Problem solved
- Offer/product
- Revenue model
- Startup cost estimate
- Time-to-launch estimate
- Required skills
- Required tools/platforms
- Suggested council ownership
- Suggested agents
- MVP scope
- First 7-day plan
- First 30-day plan
- KPIs
- Risks
- Assumptions
- Open questions
- Automation opportunities
- Suggested next action
