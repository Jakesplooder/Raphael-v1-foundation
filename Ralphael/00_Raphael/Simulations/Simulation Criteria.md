| Criterion | Meaning | Default Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| startup_cost | Higher score = lower startup cost | 1.0 |
| time_required | Higher score = less time required | 1.0 |
| difficulty | Higher score = easier | 1.0 |
| revenue_potential | Higher score = higher revenue potential | 1.5 |
| skill_fit | Higher score = better match for Aaron's skills | 1.3 |
| strategic_value | Higher score = helps long-term goals | 1.5 |
| risk | Higher score = lower risk | 1.2 |
| speed_to_launch | Higher score = faster to launch | 1.1 |
| portfolio_value | Higher score = improves public/career portfolio | 1.2 |
| automation_potential | Higher score = easier to automate with Raphael | 1.4 |
