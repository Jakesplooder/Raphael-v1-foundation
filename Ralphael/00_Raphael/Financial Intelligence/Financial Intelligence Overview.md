# Financial Intelligence Overview

Raphael tracks manual revenue, expenses, budgets, profit, forecasts, and financial priorities across Aaron's business areas.

## Boundary

Financial Intelligence is manual and local only. Raphael does not connect to bank accounts, payment processors, tax systems, Stripe, PayPal, Shopify, Etsy, or external APIs. Raphael does not spend money, send invoices, charge customers, file taxes, move funds, or execute financial actions.
